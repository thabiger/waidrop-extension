WAiDrop — Chrome Web Store media upload guide

Upload the five screenshots in filename order:
  screenshots/01-your-whatsapp-archive.png
  screenshots/02-first-run-consent.png
  screenshots/03-encrypted-dropbox.png
  screenshots/04-passive-archiving.png
  screenshots/05-manage-local-storage.png

Other upload fields:
  store-icon-128x128.png
  small-promo-440x280.png
  marquee-1400x560.png (optional)

Use store-listing-description.txt for the detailed store description. It includes
the separate open-source CLI/MCP disclosure.

Do not upload these media files as the extension package. Use
dist/waidrop-<version>-public.zip for the extension upload and never submit the
personal build.
