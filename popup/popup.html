WAiDrop

WhatsApp Chat Archiver

Before WAiDrop starts

WAiDrop reads messages, captions, media and metadata that WhatsApp Web has already delivered to your browser. It stores them in an encrypted archive on this device. If you connect Dropbox, it uploads encrypted objects to your own Dropbox app folder. The developer never receives your archive, Dropbox token or encryption key.

WAiDrop does not send messages, mark chats as read, or automate WhatsApp. Disappearing/self-destructing media is not captured. You must save your encryption key; if you lose it, the archive cannot be recovered.
